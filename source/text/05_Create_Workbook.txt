This toolkit is a working toolkit, a formalized process of how a few communities have bettered themselves through designing, building and maintaining their own park. As
a working toolkit, it is a work in progress. It is not perfect, but it is a great start towards a complex issue distilled into a few chapters. This is the first of several versions that
will be published. Behind the scenes many parks are being built, communities bettering themselves, and people reviewing this process. Each park built, each community
meeting, and every trip to hardware store - something is learned. All this knowledge gained contributes to this book continuously as a feedback loop. Also crucial to the next
version is getting feedback from professionals, organizations, and most importantly, community members like you! Please share your thoughts, questions, or ideas with us!

OVERVIEW
The Create chapter is all about building your park. We broke the process down into seven
phases. Each phase can be completed in roughly a weekend. The size of the park and
number of volunteers will impact how long the park will take to build.

These phases are ordered for a reason, and ensure successful park completion. Review the
details for each of the eight phases and make adjustments to the schedule as necessary for
your own particular park requirements.

This book assumes you have done the cost estimate tool and know the quantities
and materials you will need to order for each step. If you haven’t, please go back to
the dream workbook to use the cost estimator.
