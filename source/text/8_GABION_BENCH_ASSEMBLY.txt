COMMON LUMBER SELECTIONS \\
WESTERN RED CEDAR:                                                                                                         (8) 2” x 4” x 8’
PRO: NATURALLY WEATHER/ROT RESISTANT LUMBER WITH AESTHETICALLY PLEASING RED TONES
AND PLEASANT AROMAS, THAT IS EASY TO WORK WITH. NO PRESERVATION TREATMENTS ARE
NECESSARY AS THE SURFACES WILL GRADUALLY DEVELOP A LOVELY GRAY PATINA. IT IS EASILY
RECYCLABLE AT THE END OF ITS PRIMARY USE.
CON: COST, BE SURE TO SPECIFY ‘KNOTTY’ (STK) GRADE WHICH IS MUCH MORE AFFORDABLE
THAN ‘CLEAR’ GRADE.

DOUGLAS FIR:
PRO: MODERATELY WEATHER/ROT RESISTANT AND STRONG LUMBER. IT IS ALSO EASY TO WORK
WITH AT A LOWER COST THAN CEDAR. IT IS EASILY RECYCLABLE AT THE END OF ITS PRIMARY USE.
CON: IT WILL NOT STAND THE TEST OF TIME AS WELL AS CEDAR, BUT A GREAT CHOICE ESPECIALLY
IF YOU PLAN TO PAINT IT.

PRESSURE TREATED:
PRO: WEATHER/ROT RESISTANT LUMBER AT A LOW COST.
CON: LUMBER IS OFTEN OF VARIABLE POOR QUALITY AND SATURATED WITH CHEMICAL TREATMENT
MAKING IT DIFFICULT TO WORK WITH LEADING TO INCONSISTENT RESULTS. NEEDS 1-4 MONTHS
OF AIR DRYING BEFORE PAINTING. NOT EASILY RECYCLABLE AND OFTEN ENDS UP IN LANDFILLS
AT THE END OF ITS PRIMARY USE. NOT RECOMMENDED FOR USE IN AREAS WHERE VEGETABLES
ARE GROWN.

GENERAL NOTES:
1. ORDER 5-10% MORE LUMBER DEPENDING ON OVERALL QUANTITIES TO ALLOW FOR SOME
VARIABLE LUMBER QUALITY AND INCORRECT CUTS.
2. EXTERIOR WOOD TREATMENT CAN PROLONG THE LIFE OF YOUR PARK ELEMENTS. HOWEVER, IT
IS NOT NECESSARY TO BRING MANY YEARS OF ENJOYMENT. ANY TREATMENTS MUST BE APPLIED
AND MAINTAINED PROPERLY WHICH IS A LABOR INTENSIVE PROCESS. ONE SHOULD DECIDE IF
THIS IS PRACTICAL OR NOT FOR THEIR PARK. THE PARK ELEMENTS ARE LOW COST, DURABLE,
AND EASY TO BUILD BY DESIGN AND IT MAY BE MORE PRACTICAL TO SIMPLY HAVE THE NEXT
GENERATION REBUILD THE ELEMENTS WHEN DEEMED NECESSARY. BELOW ARE RESOURCES/
LINKS FOR CONSIDERATION IF INTERESTED IN EXTERIOR WOOD TREATMENTS.
