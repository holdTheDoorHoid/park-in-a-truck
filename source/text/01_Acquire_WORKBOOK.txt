PARK IN A TRUCK TOOLKIT - WORKING VERSION 1.0                                                 Kimberlee.Douglas@jefferson.edu        ParkinaTruck@jefferson.edu

This toolkit describes the formalized process communities use to better themselves by designing, building and maintaining their own parks. This toolkit is a work
in progress that will be updated with community feedback and experience. Behind the scenes many parks are being built, communities bettering themselves,
and people reviewing this process. Each park built, each community meeting, and every trip to the garden store - something is learned. All this knowledge
gained contributes to this book. Please share your thoughts, questions, or ideas with us!
